Is it for you?

Wealth Compass suits some people better than others

● A great fit if you

  • Earn an income and want a clear picture of your cash flow, super and goals
  • Are comfortable using apps on your phone and linking your accounts
  • Are happy to give feedback and join four group sessions on Teams

● Probably not right now if you

  • Have a complex decision in front of you, like aged care, selling a business or estate planning. Personal advice will serve you better.
  • Are already an Inspired Money advice client. Ask your adviser about our two-licence offer instead. Your friends and family are very welcome to apply.

Questions

Good to know

Can I apply if a friend or family member is an Inspired Money client?

Yes, please do. Friends and family of our clients are very welcome to apply for the Founding Cohort.

Is this financial advice?

Partly. Otivo provides personal digital advice on the topics it covers, under its own Australian financial services licence. Read Otivo's Financial Services and Credit Guide before you use it. Our setup session, group sessions and Wealth Portal are general in nature and don't take into account your personal objectives, financial situation or needs. If you need personal advice beyond what Otivo covers, we'll connect you with one of our licensed advisers, which is a separate service.

Is my data safe?

Wealth Portal connects to your accounts with read-only access, so nothing can be moved or changed. We'll walk you through exactly how it works on your welcome call before you link anything.

What does "12-month minimum" mean?

The founding price is $795 for your first year, paid as $66.25 a month. You can cancel for a full refund within 14 days of accepting your place, as long as your setup session hasn't happened yet. After that, the remaining instalments for your 12 months stay payable: because the founding price is heavily discounted, we don't offer refunds or early release outside the cooling-off period. This doesn't affect your rights under the Australian Consumer Law. Full terms are provided before you join.

What happens after 12 months?

Your founding price covers your first 12 months. From year two, Wealth Compass continues at the standard price of $1,595 a year, or you can stop at that point. We'll remind you well before your renewal date.

How much time will it take?

Over the first 12 weeks: one 1:1 setup session, four group sessions on Teams of about an hour each, and a quick weekly check of your Wealth Portal. After that, the tools are yours to keep using for the rest of your 12-month subscription.

What if the 10 places fill up?

You'll go on the waitlist for our next cohort, and we'll let you know as soon as it opens.
